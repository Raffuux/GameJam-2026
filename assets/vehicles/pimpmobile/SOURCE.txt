source/pimpmobile.blend is the one editable car model. It contains a Body and
four separate wheels. The original and white UV color atlases are in source/;
the blend also packs the images. source/.gdignore prevents Godot from importing
the Blender source. The original FBX conversion had duplicate geometry and
incorrect custom normals; this blend retains one body and repaired normals.

PaintAtlas is the Body material for paintable faces. In Blender, change its
"Body Paint Color" node swatch to preview another color. The PAINT_VARIATIONS
text block lists the five palette choices. Add the interior to this blend.
After editing, export the shared game model with:

  blender -b --factory-startup --python source/export_pimpmobile.py

Run that command from the pimpmobile directory, or pass the absolute script
path from elsewhere. It updates pimpmobile.glb, the only game mesh. The Godot
scenes choose paint colors on its PaintAtlas surface:

  pimpmobile_glass.tscn                 original brown
  variants/pimpmobile_crimson_glass.tscn
  variants/pimpmobile_cobalt_glass.tscn
  variants/pimpmobile_emerald_glass.tscn
  variants/pimpmobile_pearl_glass.tscn

These five scenes all reference pimpmobile.glb. They apply
window_glass.gdshader to the WindowAtlas surface for transmissive windows.
The GLB alone retains an opaque, portable window material. Godot extracts two
atlas images beside the GLB during import; these are needed by its materials.
