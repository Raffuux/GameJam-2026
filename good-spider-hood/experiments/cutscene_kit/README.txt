CUTSCENE KIT
============
Shared by the mission cutscenes.

mission_cutscene.gd  base for m3_unc, m3_rescue and final: pauses the play mode, cinema bars, no
                     text and no radar, actors (spider models with their idle and walk), the
                     hero's walk and turns, camera shots and close-ups, numbered lines, night
                     light, Escape to skip, hand-over to the play camera.
silk.gd              silk built in code: a thread, a sagging thread, the failed web (twigs and
                     sagging threads), the ball of silk he gets tangled in, the round web that
                     holds.
