M2 TURNED DOWN CUTSCENE (MISSION 2, CLOSING)
============================================
Played in real time at the BUG OIL station, after the weaving gameplay, then straight into the
play mode. Run m2_turned_down_game.tscn (F6). About 25 seconds. Escape skips it.

What happens (lines M2-27 to M2-31)
------------
1. His heart-shaped web fell on him: Web Smith sits tangled in a ball of silk in front of the
   weaver, still smooth: "Ayo, shawty! I spun this for you!" (M2-27).
2. Close on her: "I don't want no fake weaver like you. Just bounce, spida." (M2-28). She walks
   back into the shop.
3. Through the windscreen: Bug Smoke (M2-29) and the gang (M2-30) crack up.
4. Back on him, muffled: "...Can somebody cut me down?" (M2-31).
5. Fade to black; he is out of the silk when the play camera takes over.

Files
-----
m2_turned_down_cutscene.gd  extends gas_cutscene.gd (station, car and gang, girl, camera, lines,
                            hand-over) with its own lines and shots
m2_turned_down_game.gd/.tscn play_game.tscn + the cutscene
The ball of silk comes from ../cutscene_kit/silk.gd. The voices from ../voices/.
