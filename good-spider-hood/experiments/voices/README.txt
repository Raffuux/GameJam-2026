VOICES
======
The recorded lines of the script (Game Jam: dialogues/script-v2.md, audio/script-voix.md), one
file per numbered line, named <number>_<who> (.ogg, .wav or .mp3): M1-01_ws1.mp3, M2-15_girl.mp3...
The cutscenes ask voice_lines.gd for a line by its number and play it; a missing file is a silent
line of its fallback length.

To use another take, give it the plain name: M1-05_ws2_b.mp3 becomes M1-05_ws2.mp3.
Not recorded yet: M2-01 to M2-10 (the sunset scene with Bug Smoke).
Barks during gameplay (M2-23 to M2-26, M3-12, M3-13) are here too, for the missions to play.
