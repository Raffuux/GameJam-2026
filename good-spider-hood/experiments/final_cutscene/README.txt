FINAL CUTSCENE
==============
Played in real time at the foot of the same rock as the rescue, then a fade to black and back to
the play mode. Run final_game.tscn (F6). About 35 seconds. Escape skips it.

What happens (lines M3-14, FIN-01 to FIN-06)
------------
1. The young spider sits safe in the round web Web Smith spun: "Yo... you spun that? But you a
   jumper!" (M3-14).
2. The girl from the gas station runs up. "It's you who saved my brother?" (FIN-01) / "Well
   yeah... I tried my best." (FIN-02).
3. "Not too bad for a jumper... I guess you can weave." (FIN-03). She walks off, smiling over her
   shoulder: "Hit my line sometime, Web Smith." (FIN-04).
4. Bug Smoke slides up beside him: "Bro... she gave you her NUMBER?!" (FIN-05).
5. Close on Web Smith, smiling: "Told y'all. Keep your eight eyes open." (FIN-06).
6. The camera rises over the rock and the picture fades to black, then back to play.

Files
-----
final_cutscene.gd   extends ../m3_rescue_cutscene/m3_rescue_cutscene.gd (the rock and the young
                    spider) with the web, the girl, Bug Smoke and its own shots
final_game.gd/.tscn play_game.tscn + the cutscene
The web comes from ../cutscene_kit/silk.gd.
