M3 UNC CUTSCENE (MISSION 3, OPENING)
====================================
Played in real time at night, at the crossing where the Tisseuses mocked him (n3_1), then
straight into the play mode. Run m3_unc_game.tscn (F6). About 65 seconds. Escape skips it.

What happens (lines M3-01 to M3-09)
------------
1. From across the street: Web Smith alone at the edge of the boulevard, at night.
2. Unc, the old wise spider with his cane, walks up behind him and sits beside him.
3. Close-ups, back and forth: "Yo Smith, you aight?" (M3-01) ... "I looked like a fool. Twice."
   (M3-04).
4. Two-shot from the street: every spida that ever spun somethin' looked like a fool first (M3-05).
5. Slow push in on Unc: Spida Jesus and his one thread (M3-06).
6. The hero listening; then Unc: silk don't spin for pride, it spins for somebody (M3-07).
7. Two-shot: you a jumper... who better to catch 'em? (M3-08).
8. Unc gets up and walks off down the boulevard into the dark, without a word.
9. Close on the hero: "...For somebody." (M3-09). He gets up; the camera becomes the play camera.

It stays night afterwards (moonlight, blue fog).
