M1 END CUTSCENE (MISSION 1, CLOSING): THE FAILED WEB
====================================================
Played in real time at the same crossing as the intro (n3_1), then straight into the play mode.
Run m1_end_game.tscn (F6). About 20 seconds. Escape skips it.

What happens (lines M1-09 to M1-12)
------------
1. Close on the web he just spun beside the boulevard: two twigs and a few sagging threads, one
   snapped. "Shit..." (M1-09).
2. Over his shoulder: the Tisseuses, still parked in their lowrider. "Damn... is that weavin'?"
   (M1-10).
3. Wide from beside the web: they laugh (M1-11), "Word. Stay bouncy, my spida." (M1-12).
4. They drive off, normally (no hydraulics), the leaves swirl behind them. He watches them go,
   looks at his web, and turns back down the boulevard; the camera becomes the play camera.

The web stays where it is afterwards.

Files
-----
m1_end_cutscene.gd   extends intro_cutscene.gd (street, lowrider and crew, camera, lines, hand-over)
                     with its own lines and shots
m1_end_game.gd/.tscn play_game.tscn + the cutscene
The web comes from ../cutscene_kit/silk.gd. The voices from ../voices/.
